2. Press coverage, summarized
-------------------------------
This reconstruction did not itself re-read press articles. It relies on a
summary produced by a prior research pass, which reported 8 or more outlets
converging on the following claims:

- Mechanism described only as "approveAndCall abuse of LayerZero delegate
  permissions on Base". No outlet named the specific composition with
  EndpointV2.setConfig, ReceiveUln302.verify and commitVerification.
- Phantom SAND minted: about 329 trillion SAND (some outlets instead cited
  14.9 billion SAND) across roughly 400 to 700 transactions over about 5
  hours.
- Amount reported as actually drained from the Ethereum OFT Adapter: about
  14.75 million SAND, about 80 ETH, about 675,000 USD.
- Bridging halted on Base and BSC after the incident.
- PeckShield and Blockaid credited with detecting the activity on August 21
  to 22.
- No attacker address or transaction hash was disclosed in any article.
- One outlet (crypto.news) described a funding wallet as "dormant for 313
  days" before the incident.

Claims from that press summary that this reconstruction could NOT
independently confirm from primary on-chain text:
- PeckShield's and Blockaid's specific involvement. No on-chain trace of
  either firm's tooling or wallet activity was found; this is a press claim
  taken at face value, not verified here.
- The "wallet dormant for 313 days" detail. The attacker's own Ethereum EOA
  (0x53eda2e80E46B804C5a47260cE04642e82d004cA) was freshly created the same
  day (first inbound transaction 2026-08-21 18:42:59 UTC, 10 lifetime
  transactions total), so it does not fit that description, and it could
  not be determined which wallet the claim actually referred to.
- The alternative "14.9 billion SAND" phantom-mint figure. It matches no
  number produced by the eth_getLogs reconstruction of Base mint events
  (which totals 329,243,083,813,775.775083 SAND across 637 distinct
  transactions in the scanned window).
- Any USD valuation. No price source for 2026-08-21/22 was checked in this
  pass; the only USD-adjacent figure used anywhere in this repo is a
  same-day sanity check against a live Blockscout ETH/USD display rate,
  explicitly marked as such, never as a historical valuation.
- Whether the Sandbox's own on-chain whitehat/ransom message (tx
  0xde26ad2e26dee314879ba257ccd73c3c810fa8e09be3704e87ded29151137969) was
  covered anywhere in press. No coverage of it was found in this pass.

Everything else in this repo, all addresses, transaction hashes, block
numbers, timestamps and token amounts, was derived from primary sources:
The Sandbox's own GitHub deployment files (thesandboxgame/sandbox-smart-contracts)
for contract addresses and owners, and direct eth_call / eth_getLogs queries

Base via mainnet.base.org and gateway.tenderly.co/public/base, BSC via
bsc-dataseed.bnbchain.org) for all on-chain state and event data. No number in
