## The three biggest

1. **MakerDAO ETH-A dormant keeper** (Ethereum), ≈ $540,600. A dormant keeper contract that had won four 2020 ETH-A liquidation auctions exposed a public function that handed a caller-supplied contract control of its Vat position, so a fresh account claimed the collateral and withdrew 200 ETH. [Full writeup →](https://github.com/RealSpap/onchain-postmortems/tree/main/makerdao-eth-a-dormant-keeper-drain/)
2. **FlashLoopAdapter Safe module** (Ethereum), ≈ $310,029. A custom Safe module for leveraged Aave positions trusted any caller that claimed to have it enabled and forwarded caller-supplied calldata to a caller-chosen address, so a fake Safe made it drain the two real Safes that had enabled it. [Full writeup →](https://github.com/RealSpap/onchain-postmortems/tree/main/flashloopadapter-safe-module-caller-spoof/)
3. **UNCX Vesting V2 (Ethereum leg)** (Ethereum), ≥ $139,853. A shared vesting pool's release and cancel paths kept share accounting inconsistent, so a fresh account claimed tokens deposited by others; three more senders followed within the hour. [Full writeup →](https://github.com/RealSpap/onchain-postmortems/tree/main/uncx-vesting-v2-share-accounting/)
