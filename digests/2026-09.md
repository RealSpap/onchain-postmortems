# On-Chain Postmortems: September 2026 Digest

**At least $185.8M lost across 22 incidents this month, each mechanism reconstructed on-chain.** $183.8M of that total is independently recomputed; 3 dollar figures ($2.0M, marked †) are taken from the protocol's own post-mortem, press or DefiLlama and not recomputed.

Each edition covers the prior complete calendar month, published once that month closes. Two hot wallet thefts dated 2026-09-24 make up most of this month's total; both are counted for their Ethereum leg only, so the month's figure is a floor.

## The three biggest

1. **Bitget hot wallet drain (Ethereum leg)** (Ethereum), ≥ $126,417,066. Spoofed withdrawal requests signed by Bitget's own hot wallets; the issuer-freezable $60.4M of USDT, USDC and XAUt was sold for ETH through signed orders within 30 minutes. [Full writeup →](https://github.com/RealSpap/onchain-postmortems/tree/main/bitget-hot-wallet-spoofed-withdrawals/)
2. **Liquid Network** (Bitcoin + Liquid), ≥ $46,568,719. Range-proof cache-key collision in Elements let a peg-out register as fully backed; the federation's real multisig then genuinely signed the BTC release. [Full writeup →](https://github.com/RealSpap/onchain-postmortems/tree/main/liquid-rangeproof-cache/)
3. **Duelbits hot wallet drain (Ethereum leg)** (Ethereum), ≥ $3,020,666. Five transfers signed by Duelbits's own hot wallet after a suspected key compromise; the same address then collected ETH bridged from Tron and a second chain, and the freezable USDT and USDC were swapped within 44 minutes. [Full writeup →](https://github.com/RealSpap/onchain-postmortems/tree/main/duelbits-hot-wallet-drain/)
