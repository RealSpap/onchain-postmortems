# On-Chain Postmortems: August 2026 Digest

**At least $47.6M lost across 19 incidents this month, each mechanism reconstructed on-chain.** $12.9M of that total is independently recomputed; 8 dollar figures ($34.7M, marked †) are taken from the protocol's own post-mortem, press or DefiLlama and not recomputed.

This is the first entry in a new monthly recurring format. Each edition covers the prior complete calendar month, published once that month closes.

## The three biggest

1. **Tectonic** (Cronos), † $9,190,000. Donation attack (recursive collateral minting then direct donations to the market contract) inflating an exchange rate, massive borrowing against the inflated collateral, followed by a chain rollback; $9.19M had left Cronos before the halt, per Cronos's official post-mortem. [Full writeup →](https://github.com/RealSpap/onchain-postmortems/tree/main/tectonic-cronos/)
2. **Moonwell (MAMO market)** (Base), † $9,131,000. Donation attack on an illiquid market's exchange rate, combined with collateral/oracle manipulation. [Full writeup →](https://github.com/RealSpap/onchain-postmortems/tree/main/moonwell-mamo-oracle/)
3. **Term Finance (Meta Vault)** (Ethereum), † $8,500,000. Hijacked governance: a proposal self-voted by a fresh wallet through an attacker-deployed executor, normal 6-day delay elapsed with no veto. [Full writeup →](https://github.com/RealSpap/onchain-postmortems/tree/main/termfinance-metavault-governance/)
