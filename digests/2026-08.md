**At least $46.7M lost across 19 independently verified incidents this month.** Every figure traced hop-by-hop on-chain, not reprinted from a press release or a third-party tracker.

This is the first entry in a new monthly recurring format. Each edition covers the prior complete calendar month, published once that month closes.

## The three biggest

1. **Moonwell (MAMO market)** (Base), ≈ $9,131,000. Donation attack on an illiquid market's exchange rate, combined with collateral/oracle manipulation. [Full writeup →](https://github.com/RealSpap/onchain-postmortems/tree/main/moonwell-mamo-oracle/)
2. **Term Finance (Meta Vault)** (Ethereum), ≈ $8,500,000. Hijacked governance: a proposal self-voted by a fresh wallet through an attacker-deployed executor, normal 6-day delay elapsed with no veto. [Full writeup →](https://github.com/RealSpap/onchain-postmortems/tree/main/termfinance-metavault-governance/)
3. **Tectonic** (Cronos), ≈ $8,300,000. Donation attack (recursive collateral minting then direct donations to the market contract) inflating an exchange rate, massive borrowing against the inflated collateral, followed by a chain rollback. [Full writeup →](https://github.com/RealSpap/onchain-postmortems/tree/main/tectonic-cronos/)
