carried ($120M+ borrowed, ~11,000 blocks, $8.3M unrecoverable), which

what existed at the time; this is a normal update as better primary
information became available, the same pattern already documented for
several other entries in this repo (e.g. Ostium, where 3 conflicting
press estimates were later settled by the protocol's own investigation).

Note on the earlier press figures above ($75M borrowed, $6M escaped):
these are from August 31, the day after the exploit, before Cronos's
own investigation concluded. The official 2026-09-08 post-mortem's
$120.4M and $9.19M figures are more complete and are what this entry
now uses; the August 31 figures are kept here only as a record of what
was reported in the first 24 hours, not as competing current claims.
