  0x3157d422cd1be13AC4a7cb00957ed717e648DFf2 is a different contract,
  ERC20PermitHandler, same OpenGSN base, same execute() shape.

The two lines that matter, ERC20MetaHTLCHandler.sol lines 221 to 229
(289 lines total in the verified source):

  function execute(ForwardRequest calldata request, bytes32 domainSeparator,
                   bytes32 requestTypeHash, bytes calldata suffixData,
                   bytes calldata signature)
      public override payable onlyRelayHub returns (bool, bytes memory) {
      (request, domainSeparator, requestTypeHash, suffixData, signature);
      ...
      openPrivate(request.from, openRequestData);

The first statement of the body is a no-op that exists only to silence the
unused-parameter warning. The signature is never read. Authentication for
this call path lives in preRelayedCall, which is the paymaster half of the
same contract, and which a relay call is free to point somewhere else.

ERC20PermitHandler.sol lines 122 to 134 carry the identical pattern, ending
in transferPrivate(request.from, requestData).

6. PRESS
--------
coinfomania.com/nimiq-reports-50463-loss-due-to-exploit, read 2026-09-21
after this reconstruction was finished, not before. It gives 50463 as the
loss, credits a SlowMist alert, and describes the flaw as "the
ERC20PermitHTLCHandler's execute function, which failed to validate crucial
parameters". No address, no transaction hash, no chain named in the body.
ERC20PermitHTLCHandler is not a contract that exists at either drained
address: the drained ones are ERC20MetaHTLCHandler, and ERC20PermitHandler
is the separate transfer contract.

okx.com orbit insight 87713771195840, relaying Defimon Alerts, read the
same way. It is the most accurate public account: it names the paymaster
and forwarder doubling up, the missing signature check in execute, the
attacker's own relay, the forged opens from the liquidity wallet, the
preimage hash lock, and the three tokens. It gives no address, no hash, no
amount breakdown, and treats the whole thing as one event rather than two
transactions 105 seconds apart.

Nothing found in any source reports: that the signature field was 65 zero
bytes rather than a forgery, that the amounts are the wallet's exact
balances to the last unit, that the entry price was a 1 POL relay stake
that is still sitting in the StakeManager, that the operation ran on an
EIP-7702 delegated EOA, that four attempts failed on gas first, that the
forged opens leave no event in the contract's own log, or where the money
went.
