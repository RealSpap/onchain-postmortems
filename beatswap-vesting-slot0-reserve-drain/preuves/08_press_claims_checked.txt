CLAIM-BY-CLAIM
1. "Loss of approximately 2.985 million BTX (about $77,500)" (SlowMist via PANews / Gate: 2,984,557 BTX, US$ 77,512)
   CHAIN: 2,984,557.865885 BTX is what the attacker sold in one leg (log 47) for 77,512.640594 USDT (log 48). It is
   not what the victims lost and not what the attacker kept.
   - Victims lost 3,072,493.276543 BTX (V1 1,240,403.080996 + V2 1,832,090.195547), reserve drop at block 120873720.
   - Attacker kept 63,704.837357 USDT (log 54), after buying back BTX for 48,750.848699 USDT and paying 12,000 USDT
     of deposits. DefiLlama's 77,512 is the gross proceeds of one sale.
   VERDICT: figure is real but measures the gross resale leg; corrected.

2. "The attacker borrowed 6 million BTX via a flash loan and sold them into the Uniswap V3 pool"
   CHAIN: 6,000,000 BTX in (log 2) and 6,000,000 BTX repaid (log 42) to 0x238a3588...e6c4. The dumps were
   4,038,419.593256 BTX (log 4) and 1,674,447.967473 BTX (log 22) into 0xa5db84d7...a96d, a PancakeSwap V3 pool
   (factory 0x0BFbCF9f...1865 matches PancakeSwap's own bscMainnet.json, preuves/08b), not a Uniswap V3 pool.
   A separate 15,950 USDT flash loan (log 1 / log 51) is not mentioned by press.
   VERDICT: substantially right; venue mislabelled (PancakeSwap V3, a Uniswap V3 fork).

3. "depositing USDT in two separate transactions to trigger liquidity minting"
   CHAIN: both deposit() calls (10,000 USDT to V1 at log 7, 2,000 USDT to V2 at log 25) are inside the single
   transaction 0xcc71a3bb...5799. The attacker EOA's nonce was 0 before it and 1 after.
   VERDICT: incorrect; one transaction.

4. "withdrew BTX from the LP positions" / "draining funds from liquidity provider positions"
   CHAIN: the LP NFTs 7392248 and 7392249 were never withdrawn. They are still owned by the two vesting contracts with
   their liquidity intact (ownerOf and positions() at latest). The BTX came out by swapping back through the pool
   (log 39: 8,779,925.426614 BTX out for 48,750.848699 USDT in), which converted the in-range victim positions to
   USDT. They now hold about 7,049.38 USDT.
   VERDICT: incorrect; the positions were traded against, not withdrawn.

5. Date: "September 11, 2026" appears in search summaries of SlowMist's report; Gate's article is dated 12/09/2026.
   CHAIN: block 120873720 is 2026-09-09 11:54:23 UTC. DefiLlama's date (1788912000 = 2026-09-09) is right.
   VERDICT: report date, not incident date.

6. DeFiHackLabs "~$63.7K" attacker profit
   CHAIN: 63,704.837357 USDT to the EOA. Matches.

7. DefiLlama classification "Oracle Manipulation / Spot Price Manipulation"
   CHAIN: _calculateQuote reads TARGET_POOL.slot0() once and sizes the BTX leg from it (preuves/02 lines 347-357).
   VERDICT: accurate.
