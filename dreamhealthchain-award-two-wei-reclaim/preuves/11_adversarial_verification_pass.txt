Adversarial verification pass, Dream Health Chain award-reclaim entry
Run 2026-09-17, against the draft written the same day.

The rule applied here: a headline figure that is a SUM across many transfers is not a verified
fact just because the query ran. Every headline claim below was re-derived by a second method
that does not share the first one's failure mode, and the largest single contributor to each
sum was pulled up and looked at on its own.

1. "The award contract lost 568,370.746789 DHC"
   Method A (aggregate): sum of the 778 Transfer events in the four attacker receipts, netting
     the award contract's side. Result 568,370.746788580565912400 wei.
   Method B (no aggregation at all): two balanceOf reads, at block 120055459 and 120055460, from
     two independent archive providers (blastapi, nodereal), both returning the same values:
     568,371.829157042339390458 then 1.082368461773454472. Delta 568,370.746788580565935986.
   The two differ by 23,586 wei, that is 2.4e-14 DHC. DHC is a reflection token, so balances
     move without emitting a Transfer; the residue is consistent with that and far too small to
     touch any figure published here. Both are quoted, neither is rounded into the other.
   Largest contributor, checked on its own: the 18 payouts of 21,441.825759844717792800 DHC
     (385,952.86 DHC, half the total out). Each is a Transfer from the award contract to the
     attacker contract inside transaction 0x037771c0..., which is one of the four transactions
     sent by the attacker EOA in block 120055460. There is no time window, no address list and
     no "from any sender" clause in this filter: the set is four transactions from one sender in
     one block, so it cannot absorb unrelated traffic the way a busy-address query can.

2. "The attacker realised 71,851.016708 USDT"
   Method A: net of the USDT Transfer events across the four receipts for the EOA.
   Method B: balanceOf(EOA) on USDT, which is 0 at block 120055459 and 71,851.016708262373165555
     at block 120055460, from the same two archive providers, plus a third (bsc-dataseed) at the
     latest block. Nonce 0 before, 4 after. Exact agreement, to the wei.
   This is the figure DefiLlama tracks as $71,800 and the DeFiHackLabs PoC as "~71,851 USDT".
     It is not disputed here, only relabelled: it is the cash-out, not the victim's loss.

3. "333 claims across 5 positions, 261 of them on one position"
   Method A (used in the draft): the call traces, counting calls to selector 0x43609f36.
   Method B (used in the published script): the receipts' own Transfer events, grouped by amount,
     with no reference to the traces at all. Both give 333 payouts and the same five amounts,
     with 261 at 1,401.651994482133172200 DHC. preuves/10_claim_ledger_from_logs.txt is method B
     printed transaction by transaction.
   The grouping key is the payout amount, so two positions opened for the identical amount would
     be merged. Here the five amounts differ by orders of magnitude, and the five opening
     participations match them one for one at exactly 84.74%, so the grouping is not hiding a
     sixth position.

4. "Each claim pays 10% of the declared amount, and 328 of the 333 participations credited zero"
   Checked both ways: the traces show the participate calls carrying 2 wei after the first, and
     the receipts show 328 Transfer events into the contract with value exactly 0. The declared
     amount itself never appears in a log, so the script recovers it as payout x 10 and then
     cross-checks it against the opening leg the contract was actually credited; all five land on
     84.740000% to eight digits, which a wrong reconstruction of the declared amount would not do.

5. "The implementation was never replaced, the balance never refilled, the USDT never moved"
   Direct single reads, no aggregation, from three providers at the latest block. All agree.
   These are the time-sensitive claims in this entry: they are true as of block 122476848
     (2026-09-17). They are stated with that block in the README and have to be re-read before
     being repeated anywhere.

WHAT THIS PASS COULD NOT CONFIRM, and which is therefore hedged or left out of the entry

a. The identity of 0x590162bf4b50F6576a459B75309eE21D92178A10 and the relay
   0xF82d00fC51F730F42A00F85E74895a2849ffF2Dd. The funding transaction's shape (an unshield
   followed by a WBNB withdrawal and an internal 0.09975 BNB transfer to a nonce-0 wallet) is
   read directly from the receipt and the call trace, and it is the shape of a privacy pool.
   The commonly used label for these addresses was not confirmed against a primary deployment
   record, so the entry describes the behaviour and gives the addresses, and does not name a
   protocol.

b. The award contract's real function names and its state machine. The implementation
   (0x5abb3fe2...) is not verified on BscScan, and none of 0x7f200fee, 0xe3db9b54, 0x43609f36
   resolve in openchain.xyz's signature database. The names used in the public PoC
   (createAward, participateAward, claimAward) do not hash to those selectors, under any of the
   obvious argument-type variants that were tried. So the entry names them by selector and
   describes only what they observably do. The "status 2 reset to status 1" description in the
   PoC is not reproduced here, because nothing in the chain data shows a status field.

c. Who else had an open award position. Enumerating every historical depositor would need a full
   log scan back to the proxy's 2022-03-15 deployment (block 16080032), which the 50,000-block
   getLogs limit on the available providers makes expensive; it was not done. The entry therefore
   says what the balance shows, that the contract can no longer pay anyone, and does not put a
   number on how many people that is.

d. The 5% of each declared amount that is never pulled from the caller at all. The 84.74% leg
   into the contract is measured on all five positions; the 10.26% leg to the caller's own
   inviter is measured on the first position's opening call only (trace of 0xd65c82da...,
   1,438.094946338668634754 DHC against a declared 14,016.519944821331722750). Whether the
   remaining 5% is the token's transfer tax or the award contract's own arithmetic is not
   determined here; both legs are quoted as measured, not as a rule.
