Press coverage found (web search, 2026-09-09), none of which publishes any
on-chain address, transaction hash, or block number:

- Yahoo Finance, "Another DeFi Hack: Term Labs Loses $8.5 Million in
  Governance Exploit": attacker "cheaply accumulated a majority position
  in a sparsely held governance token", proposal "submitted on August 17"
  and "executed after roughly six days" against a nominal seven-day delay
  with LP veto rights; ~2,843 ETH (~$6.87M) + ~1.68M USDC drained, ~68% of
  $12.45M in Term's Meta Vaults, "nearly all of its Ethereum deposits."
- CrowdfundInsider, "Ethereum Based DeFi Lending Protocol Term Finance
  Suffers Setback...": corroborates the ~2,843 ETH / ~1.68M USDC figures
  and the Yearn V3 + custom-governance-layer framing; explicitly states no
  on-chain identifiers were disclosed in the piece.
- w3rooster.com, "When Governance Becomes the Attack Surface...": gives a
  competing account of the delay mechanism - "a queued proposal executed
  after the voting period ended on August 23 with no vetoes" (i.e. the
  normal delay ran its course, nobody vetoed), transferring "approximately
  2,841.74 WETH from the ETH Meta Vault" and "about 1.68 million USDC from
  several USDC vaults." Explicitly states Term "has not disclosed which
  role the attacker used or why the timelock and LP veto did not prevent
  the exploit transactions."
- shattered.io, "Term Finance DeFi Hack: $8.5M Gone in Minutes": gives yet
  another account of the delay mechanism - the attacker "self-approved a
  governance proposal that reset that seven-day delay down to zero";
  states exact amounts "2,841.74 WETH and 1,679,639 USDC."
- CoinDesk, "Ethereum lending app Term Finance loses $8.5 million after
  attacker buys voting power": WebFetch's summary of this article named an
  attacker address, 0xD5183d8BfC65a50863C62aF2538198A8288FFc13, attributed
  to "PeckShield." Independently re-fetching the same URL directly with
  curl returned a "Vercel Security Checkpoint" bot-challenge page
  containing zero 0x-prefixed strings, not the article - so this
  project could NOT independently confirm that attribution from the
  primary article text. A separate WebSearch call's own synthesis of
