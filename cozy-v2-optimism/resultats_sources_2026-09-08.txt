2. Cozy Finance's own account, posted on X (@cozyfinance), 2026-09-07,
   read directly from the post (https://x.com/cozyfinance/status/2097085271867118062):

   "What happened, in brief:

   On September 2, an attacker bought protection in three Cozy v2 markets
   (Aave v2, Curve, Rabbithole Quests) and, in the same transactions,
   submitted false "YES" answers to the UMA Optimistic Oracle requests
   those markets rely on.

   The proposals were not disputed during the five-day challenge period.
   Early on September 7 they settled, the three markets triggered, and the
   attacker claimed 170,186 USDC.e from the Sets' collateral, bridged it to
   Ethereum, and deposited it into Tornado Cash within about 90 minutes.
   Attacker address: 0x003FE7359A4E03C85Ac2f521eC699ED84C7c5ccB

   A full account will be posted by Thursday, September 10, 18:00 UTC."

   A quoted, earlier post from the same account (2026-09-07, machine
   auto-translated to French in the browser, original presumed English):
   "We are aware of an exploit affecting Cozy v2 protection markets on
   Optimism. Funds were drained from the Cozy v2 Main Set and the
   Rabbithole Set. We are investigating with our security partners and
   will share details as they become available."
